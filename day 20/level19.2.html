The Lamborghini Aventador is a V12-powered sports car produced
by Lamborghini from 2011 to 2022, succeeding the Murciélago as the company's flagship model. It was known for its extreme design,
performance, and use of lightweight carbon fiber. The final version, the Aventador LP 780-4 Ultimae, was produced in 2022, after which it was replaced by the hybrid Revuelto.
